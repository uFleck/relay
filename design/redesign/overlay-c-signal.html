A status board. The collapsed tab is a 24 px strip: count on a black cap, then one colour block per workflow category sized by its task count, then sync pips. You can tell "mostly blue, one red" from across the desk. When something needs you, the whole panel header becomes an amber band.

Live · hover the strip, leave to collapse (400 ms grace)

Keeps: every count, claims, next due, needs-you, sync with retry blink, update state, DND, pin, drag, Ctrl+Alt+R. Changes: the tab and gauge show the status-category mix (same statusColor(category) map as the main window); the needs-you state is a header band, not a chip; numerals in Bahnschrift.
